Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before buying

Do you offer financing options?
Yes! We partner with leading banks and SACCOs in Kenya. You can finance up to 80% of the vehicle price with repayment periods from 12 to 60 months.
Can I trade in my old car?
Absolutely! Bring your vehicle to our showroom and our team will evaluate it and offer you a fair market price that goes toward your new car.
Do you deliver outside Nairobi?
We deliver countrywide at a small cost because your time (and fuel) is worth more than our delivery fee. We arrange delivery with a small logistics fee.
What documents do I need to buy a car?
You'll need a valid National ID or Passport, KRA PIN certificate, and proof of income (for financing). For cash purchases, just your ID and KRA PIN.
Are all vehicles inspected?
Yes — every vehicle undergoes a rigorous inspection covering engine, transmission, brakes, suspension, electronics, and body condition.
